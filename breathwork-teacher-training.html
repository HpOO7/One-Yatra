About the training

This Breathwork Teacher Training gives you the knowledge, experience and confidence to practice and facilitate breathwork sessions for yourself and others. It combines theory and practical training, and covers many breathing techniques, from beginner-friendly practices to advanced energy-clearing methods.

Course Curriculum

From foundation to facilitation mastery

Level 1 — Foundation · 15 hours

For self-practice, 1:1 sessions, and friends & family

  • 6+ core breathing techniques for mental clarity, emotional release, physical vitality and inner balance
  • Energizing breathwork to boost prana, focus and daily energy
  • Relaxing & restorative breaths to calm the mind and regulate the nervous system
  • Conscious connected circular breath for deeper emotional clearing and expanded awareness
  • Breathing tools for daily life — simple practices you can use anywhere, anytime
  • How to hold space for family and friends in basic breathwork sessions
  • Yogic philosophy & theory of breath — prana, nadis, energy flow and the purpose of breathing practices
  • Physiology of breath — how the respiratory system works and how breath affects the mind-body connection
Level 2 — Facilitation & Advanced · 30 hours

12 hours theory + 18 hours practical

  • Advanced breathwork techniques for energy activation, chakra cleansing, trauma release and deeper emotional healing
  • Ganga cold-water breathwork in the sacred waters of Ma Ganga, to awaken inner strength, clarity, resilience and pranic expansion
  • Master the art of holding space — guide one-on-one and group sessions with safety, sensitivity and energetic awareness
  • 5-element energy purification practices to cleanse, balance and protect your energy field
  • Spiritual connection & pranic awakening to deepen intuition, inner silence and connection to higher consciousness
  • Create your own breathwork sequences — design safe, impactful sessions and full classes
  • Soul numerology guidance to discover your unique energy and inner master (worth €120)
  • Activate your intuition to become a grounded, heart-centered facilitator

Meet Your Teacher

Arjun Ji

Breathwork, Pranayama & Meditation Instructor · Founder of Ekam Journey

Arjun Ji was born in Rishikesh and trained in Kriyas directly by Himalayan yogis. He shares ancient wisdom through simple, practical and transformative breath practices. He has led breathwork journeys, meditation trainings, pranayama courses and healing retreats in India, Japan, Indonesia, Myanmar and Nepal. Many teachers he trained now run their own sessions, retreats and workshops around the world. His teaching blends yogic knowledge with modern breath science.
